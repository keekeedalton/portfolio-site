## Tier S — Anchor case studies (write these first)

1. **2025 Risk Review Help Cases AI Agent** — AI-assisted resolution rate 7% → 21%+ in three months; 12% Q4 Help Cases reduction. Involved system-prompt writing and self-serve content/UX enablement. The cleanest single proof point of "AI builder + content designer" simultaneously.
2. **2025 AI Autonomy Framework** (co-authored) — Org-level framework adopted by multiple product and engineering teams. Signals design leadership of AI practice, not just IC contribution.
3. **2024 H2 HRBP Sensitive Reorgs** — 12-week delivery working with NORAM + international legal counsel; content-driven solution (not UI-driven) for legal partners under extreme constraint (legal, regulatory, multi-region). Strongest evidence of designing for high-stakes regulated work, independent of any AI angle.
4. **2025 Simplified Evidence Collection** (LaMa migration) — Collapsed 5 workflows into 1; %MAD (mean absolute deviation, an accuracy metric) improved 15% → 7.1%; page load 14+s → under 5s; deprecated a legacy tool. Multi-team alignment (QEPR, TAPS) plus AI-driven evidence suggestions.
5. **2024 H1 Pulse + MicroPulse** — Company-wide launch in 10 weeks; 77% CSAT, 7 points above benchmark; absorbed a methodology change mid-flight. Visibility across VPs, Managers, HRBPs, and ICs — proof of shipping at scale with speed and quality together.

## Tier A — Strong supporting case studies

6. **2023 H2 — Trained iLlama on Content Design standards via Metamate** — The explicit bridge case study for the AI-builder narrative: wrote prompts, trained on labeled examples, provided testing notes, predating the org's broader AI pivot. Strength depends entirely on how much real detail (prompts, before/after outputs) can be recovered — with detail, this could earn Tier S.
7. **2023 H2 Annual Planning 0-to-1** — Complete 0-to-1 product: 1,000+ hours saved, 3,327 position changes processed. End-to-end product surface — flows, error states, new-user experience, emails, terminology, support content.
8. **2025 Unified Manual Reviews** — 10+ obscure risk processes unified into one workflow in 3 weeks. Status system, multi-team alignment (two engineering teams, multiple PMs, two PDs, design leadership). Strong systems-thinking proof point.
9. **2024 H1 Finance Vision 2030** — Leadership-facing visioning deck, 100+ slides plus a sizzle reel, used to justify investment decisions. A different muscle from product UI work — narrative, vision, executive audience.
